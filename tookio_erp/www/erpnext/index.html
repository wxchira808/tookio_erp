The 10 Core Functional Modules in ERPNext

A complete operational ecosystem covering every department from shop floor work centers to balance sheet consolidation.

1. Manufacturing & MRP

Hierarchical multi-level BOMs, capacity scheduling, operator job cards, workstation hourly rates, and raw material scrap costing.

2. Stock & Warehousing

Multi-location warehouse trees, batch/serial tracking, FEFO/FIFO valuation, landed cost allocation, and barcode receiving.

3. General Ledger & Accounts

Direct KRA eTIMS V2 cryptographic signing, double-entry GL, automated bank/M-Pesa reconciliation, and multi-cost center P&L.

4. Buying & Procurement

Material requisitions, supplier quotation comparisons, automated 3-way matching (PO → GRN → Invoice), and vendor scorecards.

5. Selling & Distribution

Customer quotations, sales orders, price list management, debtor credit limit holds, and dispatch route manifests.

6. Quality Assurance (QA)

Mandatory inspection checkpoints for incoming goods, in-process WIP, and finished items with Certificate of Analysis (COA) generation.

7. Asset & Maintenance

Automated monthly depreciation schedules, asset tracking, preventive maintenance schedules, and machine breakdown logs.

8. HRMS & Payroll

Kenyan statutory deductions (PAYE, NHIF/SHA, NSSF, Housing Levy), biometric attendance integration, leave, and expense claims.

9. CRM & Opportunities

Lead tracking, communication history, quotation conversion tracking, and deal pipeline forecasting.

10. Custom Apps & REST APIs

Full extensibility with custom Frappe apps, Git versioning, webhooks, and REST/GraphQL APIs for banking and IoT integrations.
