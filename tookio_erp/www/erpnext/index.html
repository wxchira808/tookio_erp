The 12 Core Functional Modules in ERPNext

A complete operational ecosystem covering every department from shop floor work centers to balance sheet consolidation.

1. Accounting & Finance

Customizable chart of accounts, multi-currency ledger, bank reconciliation statement, payment entries, cost centers, and automated financial statements.

2. Stock & Warehousing

Inter-warehouse stock transfers, multi-site warehouse trees, batch & serial tracking, FEFO/FIFO valuation, landed cost vouchers, and cycle count reconciliation.

3. Selling & Order Fulfillment

Customer quotations, sales orders, price list management, customer credit limits, product bundles, delivery notes, and sales analytics.

4. Buying & Procurement

Material requisitions, supplier quotation comparison grids, automated 3-way matching (PO → GRN → Invoice), and vendor performance scorecards.

5. Point of Sale (POS) & Retail

Touch-screen cashier billing, barcode scanning, split payments (M-Pesa, card, cash), cashier shift opening/closing, and real-time branch stock deduction.

6. CRM & Sales Pipeline

Lead tracking, multi-stage opportunity funnels, 2-way email sync, appointment scheduling, and complete Customer 360 view.

7. HRMS, Attendance & Payroll

Biometric attendance sync, shift allocation, leave management, expense claims, automated formula-driven payroll, and employee self-service portal.

8. Manufacturing & MRP

Hierarchical multi-level BOMs, Production Plans, shop floor operator job cards, workstation hourly costing, scrap tracking, and subcontracting.

9. Projects & Timesheets

Gantt charts, task scheduling, timesheet logging, billable vs non-billable tracking, progressive milestone invoicing, and real-time project P&L.

10. Fixed Asset Management

Capital asset tracking, automated monthly depreciation schedules (Straight Line, WDV), asset transfer logs, and preventive maintenance schedules.

11. Quality Assurance & Inspection

Inspection checkpoints for incoming purchase receipts, in-process job cards, and delivery notes with non-conformance (NCR) and COA generation.

12. Helpdesk & SLA Support

Issue ticketing from email/portal, First Response Time and Resolution SLAs, serialized warranty claim tracking, and customer knowledge base.
