ERP Implementation Services & Methodology

We turn complex operations into disciplined digital workflows. Our structured 4-phase delivery framework eliminates go-live surprises, ensuring complete user adoption and fast return on investment.

From Operational Discovery to Live Production

Every project is managed by senior ERP engineers following a milestone-based delivery structure.

Phase 1 Week 1

Discovery & Blueprinting

On-site plant walk-throughs, stakeholder interviews, current-state gap analysis, and delivery of the comprehensive Solution Blueprint document.

  • Process flow diagrams
  • Chart of accounts design
Phase 2 Weeks 2–3

Configuration & Build

Deployment of the dedicated ERPNext instance, multi-level BOM setup, custom scripts, role permissions, and KRA eTIMS V2 API connection.

  • Custom PDF print formats
  • Approval threshold rules
Phase 3 Week 4

Migration & Enablement

Sanitizing and migrating opening balances, item catalogs, and customer data. Hands-on role-based training and user acceptance testing (UAT).

  • Opening stock valuation
  • End-to-end dry run tests
Phase 4 Week 5+

Go-Live & Hypercare

Production cutover with on-site engineering presence, daily reconciliation audits, and smooth transition to our ongoing SLA support desk.

  • Daily closing assistance
  • Quarterly performance reviews

Enterprise SLA & Support Contracts

Ensure mission-critical uptime, regular security patches, and rapid issue resolution with our dedicated Nairobi engineering support desk.

Guaranteed Incident Response Times

Defined response SLAs for critical plant floor stoppages, invoice transmission blockers, and system exceptions.

Proactive Health & Security Audits

Automated database performance tuning, index optimization, disk space monitoring, and encrypted off-site backups.

Continuous Workflow Optimization

Ongoing custom script updates, new report creation, and process enhancements as your business evolves.
