Privacy Policy

How Tookio Solutions collects, handles, and protects your information when you engage our consulting services, website, or enterprise systems.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver enterprise ERP consulting, scoping audits, and implementation services. This includes company contact details, operational requirements submitted through forms, and technical diagnostics provided during system migrations.

2. How We Use Information

Information provided is used strictly to respond to scoping inquiries, prepare Solution Blueprints, execute agreed implementation milestones, and deliver ongoing SLA support. We do not sell or monetize client data.

3. Data Security & Sovereignty

We apply enterprise-grade encryption and access controls. In production client deployments, data resides in the client's designated private cloud or on-premise infrastructure under client data sovereignty.

4. Contacting Our Data Officer

If you have questions regarding data retention, audits, or wish to update your records, contact our engineering office at hello@tookio.co.ke.
