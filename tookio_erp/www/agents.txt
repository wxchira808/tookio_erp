# Tookio Solutions — Enterprise ERP & Manufacturing Systems in Kenya

> Tookio Solutions is an enterprise ERP implementation, engineering, and digital transformation partner based in Nairobi, Kenya. We specialize in deploying, customizing, and scaling ERPNext for mid-market and large manufacturing enterprises, FMCG distributors, and supply chain operators across East Africa.

## Core Specializations
- **Manufacturing ERP**: Multi-level Bill of Materials (BOM), Work Orders, Production Planning (MRP), Shop Floor Terminals, Real-Time Yield & Scrap Analysis.
- **Inventory & Warehouse Logistics**: Multi-warehouse stock ledger, automated batch tracking, serial numbers, reorder level triggers, barcode scanning.
- **Financial Governance & KRA eTIMS V2**: Complete statutory compliance with automated electronic tax invoice transmission to the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA eTIMS V2), automated withholding tax, and localized general ledger.
- **Procurement & Supply Chain**: Supplier portals, RFQ comparison matrix, Landed Cost calculations (import duties, clearing fees, freight allocation), purchase order approvals.
- **Enterprise Integrations & Automation**: Machine IoT telemetry, weighing scale integrations, M-Pesa B2B / C2B automated reconciliation, n8n workflow pipelines, and custom Python/Frappe engineering.
