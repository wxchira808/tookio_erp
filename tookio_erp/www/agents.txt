# Tookio Solutions — All-in-One ERPNext Implementation Partner in Kenya

> Tookio Solutions is an enterprise ERP implementation, engineering, and digital transformation partner based in Nairobi, Kenya. We specialize in deploying, customizing, and scaling ERPNext for wholesalers, distributors, retail chains, service providers, and manufacturing enterprises across East Africa.

## Core ERPNext Modules Supported
- **Accounting & Financial Management**: Chart of accounts, double-entry general ledger, multi-currency accounting, automated bank & M-Pesa reconciliation, multi-cost centers, and real-time financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow).
- **Stock & Inventory Management**: Perpetual multi-warehouse inventory, inter-warehouse transfers, batch & serial tracking, FEFO/FIFO valuation, landed cost allocation, auto-reorder levels, and barcode scanning.
- **Selling & Quotations**: Customer quotations, sales orders, blanket agreements, multi-tier price lists, credit limits, product bundles, and delivery notes.
- **Buying & Procurement**: Material requisitions, supplier RFQs, quotation comparison matrices, purchase orders, automated 3-way matching, and vendor scorecards.
- **Point of Sale (POS) & Retail**: Fast counter checkout, touch-screen UI, barcode scanning, split payments (M-Pesa, card, cash), cashier shift opening/closing, and branch stock sync.
- **CRM & Sales Pipeline**: Lead management, visual deal pipelines, customer 360 timeline, 2-way email sync, appointment scheduling, and automated lead routing.
- **HRMS, Attendance & Payroll**: Digital employee records, biometric attendance sync, shift scheduling, leave approvals, formula-driven payroll, statutory deductions, and employee mobile self-service.
- **Manufacturing & MRP**: Multi-level BOMs, Production Plans, shop floor operator job cards, workstation costing, scrap tracking, and subcontracting.
- **Projects & Timesheets**: Interactive Gantt charts, task scheduling, timesheet logging, billable vs non-billable hours, progressive milestone invoicing, and project profitability.
- **Fixed Asset Management**: Capital asset registers, automated depreciation schedules (Straight Line, WDV), asset transfer tracking, and preventive maintenance logs.
- **Quality Management**: 3-stage inspection checkpoints (receiving, WIP, dispatch), non-conformance (NCR) tracking, CAPA, and Certificate of Analysis (COA).
- **Helpdesk & Support**: Support ticket workflows, First Response and Resolution SLAs, serialized warranty claim tracking, and self-service knowledge base.
