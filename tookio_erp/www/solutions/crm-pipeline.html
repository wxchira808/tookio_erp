Complete CRM Module Functionality & Standard Reports

CRM DocTypes & Pipeline

  • Lead Management: Capture leads automatically from website contact forms, inbound emails, WhatsApp, and trade shows.
  • Opportunities & Deals: Multi-stage sales deals with deal size, closing probability, source campaign, and competitor profiles.
  • Prospects & Contacts: Hierarchical organization accounts with multiple contacts, phone numbers, designations, and notes.
  • Customer 360 View: Unified dashboard tracking quotations, sales orders, pending invoices, delivery notes, and support tickets for a client.
  • Appointments & Tasks: Schedule client demo meetings, plant tours, and follow-up reminders with calendar notifications.
  • Marketing Campaigns: Track lead generation cost and ROI across Google Ads, LinkedIn campaigns, email blasts, and events.

Automation & Communication Tools

  • Automated Lead Assignment Rules: Distribute new leads via round-robin, by geographical territory, or by item group interests.
  • 2-Way Email Synchronization: Automatic IMAP/SMTP sync attaching every sent and received email to the lead or deal thread.
  • SLA Follow-Up Alerts: Automated reminders when a lead has not received a response within the mandated target window.
  • Email Templates & Mail Merge: Pre-approved sales email templates with personalized tags for fast prospect outreach.
  • One-Click Quotation Creation: Convert qualified opportunities into customer quotations with item rows pre-populated.
  • WhatsApp & SMS Integration: Send automated quotation links and order updates directly to customer mobile phones.

Built-In CRM Reports

  • Sales Funnel Report: Visual funnel showing count and monetary volume of leads progressing through each pipeline stage.
  • Lead Conversion Time: Measure average days taken from initial lead inquiry to won sales order by rep and lead source.
  • Opportunity Summary by Stage: Comprehensive pipeline forecast showing weighted deal value by expected closing month.
  • Lost Opportunity Reasons Analysis: Categorize lost deals (price too high, competitor selected, budget canceled) to refine sales strategy.
  • Campaign Efficiency Report: Audit lead volume, customer acquisitions, and cost-per-lead generated per marketing channel.
  • First Response Time for Opportunities: Measure responsiveness of sales representatives to inbound inquiries.
  • Sales Person Activity Log: Summary of calls, emails, and meetings logged per sales executive.
