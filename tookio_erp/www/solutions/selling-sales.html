Complete Selling Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every sales transaction DocType, credit control, and revenue analytics report in the ERPNext Selling suite.

Sales DocTypes & Orders

  • Customer Quotations: Generate professional PDF/web quotes with item images, payment terms, and validity expiry dates.
  • Sales Orders: Confirmed customer orders with reserved warehouse inventory, expected delivery dates, and shipping terms.
  • Blanket Orders: Manage long-term customer volume contracts with fixed negotiated unit rates across staggered delivery orders.
  • Price Lists & Pricing Rules: Unlimited price lists (Wholesale, Retail, Export, VIP) with volume tiering, margin rules, and promotions.
  • Product Bundles (Sales Kits): Sell multi-item kits under a parent bundle while auto-deducting individual stock items on delivery.
  • Sales Returns & Credit Notes: Process returns of delivered goods linked to original delivery notes and invoices with restock tracking.

Credit, Margins & Fulfillment Controls

  • Customer Credit Limit Enforcement: Set total credit amount and credit days per customer; automatically block submission when breached.
  • Margin & Minimum Selling Price: Stop sales reps from quoting below cost; require manager overrides for discounts exceeding limits.
  • Sales Partner & Rep Commissions: Automatically allocate commissions to internal reps or external referral partners upon payment realization.
  • Drop Shipping: Trigger direct-from-vendor Purchase Orders from customer Sales Orders without stocking intermediate warehouse inventory.
  • Self-Service Customer Portal: Enable B2B clients to view order status, download invoices, print account statements, and request quotes online.
  • Dispatch Route Management: Group sales orders by delivery vehicle and driver route for efficient fleet dispatching.

Built-In Selling Reports

  • Sales Analytics: Multi-dimensional revenue trends by Item Group, Customer, Territory, Brand, and Sales Person over monthly periods.
  • Sales Order Analysis: Track order fulfillment progress (Percent Delivered, Percent Billed, Percent Pending).
  • Ordered Items To Be Delivered: Warehouse dispatch backlog report showing all line items committed to customers.
  • Delivered Items To Be Billed: Shipments dispatched to clients that have not yet had accounts receivable invoices generated.
  • Gross Profit by Sales Person: Audit net revenue and gross profit margins generated per team member to reward top performers.
  • Quotation Trends: Measure quote-to-order win rates, average closing days, and lost reason codes.
  • Inactive Customers Report: Identify previously active accounts that have stopped placing orders to trigger proactive outreach.
