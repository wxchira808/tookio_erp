Built for Industrial Shop Floors & Continuous Operations

Tookio connects physical workstations, extrusion machines, and assembly lines directly to your ERP general ledger. Operators log start/stop times and material consumption using simple tablet interfaces.

  • Workstation Downtime Logs: Capture electrical outages, mold changes, and mechanical maintenance.
  • Regrind & Scrap Accounting: Automatically calculate scrap ratios and return recycled offcuts back into raw stock.
  • Actual vs Standard Costing: Compare theoretical BOM costs with real WIP material and energy consumption.

Key Capabilities of Tookio Manufacturing ERP

Designed specifically to resolve operational friction in process and discrete manufacturing.

Structured Bill of Materials (BOM)

Build hierarchical, multi-level BOMs with precise raw material ratios, packaging components, scrap percentage tolerances, and alternative item substitutes.

  • Variant BOMs for customized product lines
  • Automated revision history and change control

Material Requirements Planning (MRP)

Analyze upcoming sales orders, safety stock thresholds, and minimum reorder quantities to generate precise purchase requisitions and production orders automatically.

  • Lead-time buffer calculation and supplier POs
  • Stock shortfall early warning dashboard

Workstations & Job Cards

Assign work orders to dedicated machine workstations, record hourly operational run rates, power consumption, machine downtime, and operator labor hours.

  • Tablet-friendly shop floor terminal view
  • Real-time Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)

Batch & Serial Number Tracking

Maintain total traceability from raw material supplier heat numbers to finished goods batch codes with full genealogy reporting for compliance audits.

  • Granular backward & forward trace genealogy
  • Automatic barcode and batch label printing

Quality Assurance Checkpoints

Enforce mandatory quality checks at goods receipt, post-extrusion, assembly, and pre-dispatch with automated Certificate of Analysis (COA) generation.

  • Pass/fail tolerance thresholds and scrap logging
  • Automated quarantine warehouse routing

Preventive Maintenance & Assets

Schedule recurring machine service intervals based on operating hours or elapsed calendar time, automatically triggering maintenance work orders.

  • Spare parts inventory reservation
  • Unplanned breakdown ticket escalation
