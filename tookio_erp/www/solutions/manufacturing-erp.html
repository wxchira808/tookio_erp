Complete Manufacturing Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every manufacturing DocType, capacity control, and production report available in the ERPNext Manufacturing suite.

Manufacturing DocTypes & Routing

  • Bill of Materials (Single & Multi-Level BOM): Specify raw materials, nested sub-assemblies, operations, workstation rates, scrap allowance, and alternative items.
  • Production Plan (MRP): Auto-generate work orders for sub-assemblies and purchase requests for deficit raw materials based on sales demand.
  • Work Orders: Schedule production quantities, reserve raw materials in WIP warehouse, track start/end dates, and monitor batch completion.
  • Job Cards: Tablet-optimized operator interface to start, pause, and complete individual machine operations with barcode scanning.
  • Workstations & Operations: Set up machine centers with hourly electricity, rent, labor, and maintenance cost rates for automated costing.
  • Stock Entry for Manufacture: Issue raw materials to WIP and receive finished goods and co-products with automated GL postings.

Costing, Scrap & Capacity Controls

  • Backflushing & WIP Accounting: Automatically consume raw materials based on standard BOM ratios or record actual consumption variances.
  • Scrap & Co-Product Valuation: Track recyclable scrap, offcuts, and secondary by-products into scrap warehouses with separate valuation.
  • Capacity Planning & Scheduling: Schedule work order slots against machine operating calendars and shift capacity to eliminate bottlenecks.
  • Downtime Entry: Track machine stoppages by reason codes (tooling changes, power cuts, mechanical failure) to compute Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).
  • Subcontracting Work Orders: Transfer raw materials to third-party processors and track subcontracted service fees in finished goods valuation.
  • BOM Versioning & Item Substitution: Manage engineering change orders (ECOs) and substitute equivalent raw materials on live work orders.

Built-In Production Reports

  • Work Order Summary: Track status, planned vs produced quantities, raw material transfer status, and completion dates.
  • BOM Stock Report: Verify how many finished units can be manufactured with current warehouse raw material balances.
  • BOM Variance Report: Audit actual raw material consumed and machine time logged versus standard BOM specifications.
  • Job Card Summary: Analysis of machine operator productivity, idle time, and operation duration across work centers.
  • Production Plan Summary: High-level progress of all customer sales orders and forecast batches in production.
  • Downtime Analysis Report: Breakdown of lost production hours by machine, workstation, operator, and stoppage cause.
  • Quality Inspection Summary: Review pass/fail rates for in-process job cards and finished production batches.
