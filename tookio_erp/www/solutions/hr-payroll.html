Complete HRMS & Payroll Functionality & Standard Reports

Every HR DocType, statutory payroll engine feature, and attendance report available in the ERPNext HRMS suite.

HR Operations DocTypes

  • Employee Master: Unified personnel profile with personal info, emergency contacts, bank details, and digital contract uploads.
  • Job Openings & Recruitment: Post job openings, track applicant resumes, schedule interviews, and issue job offers.
  • Shift Types & Assignments: Day, night, and rotational shift schedules with grace period tolerances and auto-shift allocation.
  • Attendance Records: Automated check-ins from biometric machines, mobile geolocation punches, or manual daily bulk sheets.
  • Leave Applications & Allocations: Multi-tier approval workflows for annual, sick, maternity, study, and compassionate leaves.
  • Expense Claims: Mobile receipt submission for travel, fuel, or petty cash expenses with direct reimbursement through payroll or bank.

Payroll Engine & Statutory Compliance

  • Formula-Driven Salary Structures: Build flexible salary components for Basic Pay, House Allowance, Transport, Overtime, and Bonuses.
  • One-Click Bulk Payroll Processing: Process payroll for hundreds of staff by department or branch, generating individual Salary Slips and consolidated GL journals.
  • Statutory Tax Calculations: Built-in formulas for graduated PAYE income tax, NSSF Tier 1 & 2, SHA/NHIF, Housing Levy, and NITA contributions.
  • Employee Loans & Advances: Manage company staff loans with automated monthly repayment deductions amortized across salary slips.
  • Bank EFT File Generation: Export payroll payment files formatted for major banks for instant bulk electronic salary disbursement.
  • Timesheet-Based Payroll: Calculate hourly wages for contractors and factory staff based on verified job card or timesheet hours logged.

Built-In HR & Payroll Reports

  • Salary Register: Complete payroll breakdown showing earnings, statutory deductions, loan recoveries, and net pay per employee.
  • Bank Remittance Advice: Bank account numbers, employee names, and net salary figures for bulk transfer authorization.
  • Monthly Attendance Sheet: Calendar grid showing Present, Absent, Half-Day, Late-In, and On-Leave status per employee.
  • Employee Leave Balance Summary: Available, taken, and carried-forward leave balances across departments.
  • Income Tax Deductions Report: Summary of taxable pay, personal relief, insurance relief, and total PAYE withheld for tax filing.
  • Payroll Cost by Department: Analyze personnel expenditure trends across departments, cost centers, and branch locations.
  • Employee Loan Summary: Track principal disbursed, total repayments collected to date, and outstanding loan balances.
