Complete Stock Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every stock movement docType, valuation workflow, and built-in inventory report in the ERPNext Stock suite.

Stock Transactions & DocTypes

  • Inter-Warehouse Stock Transfers: Material Transfer entries between local stores and multi-step In-Transit Transfers with sender dispatch and receiver sign-off.
  • Stock Entry Types: Material Receipt, Material Issue, Material Transfer for Manufacture, Repack/Kitting, Subcontractor transfers, and Scrap entries.
  • Purchase Receipt (GRN): Verify vendor shipments against open POs, inspect accepted vs rejected quantities, and assign warehouse bins.
  • Delivery Notes & Pick Lists: Auto-generate warehouse pick lists grouped by aisle/bin and generate packing slips with gross/net weight.
  • Item Master & Item Variants: Multi-dimensional variants (Size, Color, Brand, Grade) with custom UOM conversions (e.g. Boxes to Units, Tonnes to Kg).
  • Stock Reconciliation: Upload cycle-count CSV sheets to instantly balance physical counts against system ledger with automatic variance write-offs.

Valuation & Replenishment Controls

  • Perpetual Inventory Engine: Automatic real-time accounting synchronization; every physical stock movement writes corresponding GL asset updates.
  • Valuation Methods: Choose between First-In First-Out (FIFO) and Moving Average valuation per item or warehouse category.
  • Landed Cost Allocation: Post customs duties, freight, insurance, and port handling charges across item purchase batches to capture real cost of goods sold.
  • Batch & Serial Number Tracking: Maintain complete lifecycle tracking for serialized equipment, electronics, pharmaceutical, and food batches.
  • Automated Reorder Levels: Configure minimum inventory safety stock per warehouse; system auto-triggers Material Requests before stock runs out.
  • Negative Stock Controls: Restrict negative inventory dispatches to enforce operational discipline and prevent distorted valuation rates.

Built-In Stock Reports

  • Stock Balance Report: Real-time available quantity and total valuation across each individual warehouse or company-wide.
  • Stock Ledger: Complete chronological log of every inbound, transfer, manufacturing issue, and dispatch movement with running balance.
  • Stock Projected Quantity: Calculate true stock availability (Actual Qty + Planned Incoming Purchase Orders - Reserved Sales Orders).
  • Stock Ageing Report: Identify slow-moving items and dead capital by age bracket (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days) to optimize cash flow.
  • Batch-Wise Balance History & Expiry: Track inventory balance by batch, monitor upcoming expiry dates, and conduct fast recalls.
  • Item Shortage & Reorder Report: Instant view of all items currently below safety stock thresholds across warehouses.
  • Stock Variance Report: Audit differences between recorded book stock and physical reconciliation counts.
