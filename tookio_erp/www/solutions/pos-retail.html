Complete POS Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every cashier interface feature, shift control, and retail sales report available in the ERPNext POS module.

Cashier & Counter DocTypes

  • Touch & Barcode POS Interface: Rapid item lookups by barcode scan, SKU code, or item category visual tiles.
  • POS Profile Configuration: Assign specific profiles per branch or terminal with default warehouse, price list, cash/bank accounts, and receipt layouts.
  • POS Opening Entry: Cashier records starting cash drawer float at the beginning of each retail shift.
  • POS Closing Entry: Blind cash counting at shift end; system reconciles counted cash against expected transactions and posts difference journals.
  • POS Invoices & Consolidation: Consolidate thousands of daily retail receipts into clean daily batch GL entries to keep your database lean.
  • Returns & Item Exchanges: Search previous receipt by invoice number or barcode to process full/partial returns or item exchanges.

Retail Controls & Promotions

  • Multi-Mode Split Tender: Combine Cash, M-Pesa STK Push / Till, Credit Card, and Gift Card on a single transaction.
  • Cashier Discount Limits: Restrict manual cashier price overwrites or line-item discounts without supervisor manager PIN approval.
  • Loyalty Points Accrual & Redemption: Automatic points earned per dollar spent, redeemable as instant payment deduction at the register.
  • Coupon Codes & Happy Hour Schemes: Run time-limited weekend discounts, brand promotions, and multi-buy bundling.
  • Negative Stock Prevention: Block cashier from selling out-of-stock items, or allow backorders based on management policy.
  • Hold & Resume Cart: Pause an ongoing customer transaction to serve the next customer, then retrieve the held cart instantly.

Built-In Retail Reports

  • POS Register Report: Detailed breakdown of all counter receipts filtered by Branch, Terminal, Cashier, and Payment Mode.
  • Daily Shift Cash Reconciliation: Audit actual counted drawer cash against system expected cash; highlight shortages and overages.
  • Item-Wise Sales Register: High-velocity retail item analytics showing top-selling products by quantity and revenue.
  • Branch Sales Comparison: Compare daily, weekly, and monthly retail revenue performance across branch locations.
  • Payment Method Breakdown: Real-time split percentages of Cash vs M-Pesa vs Card receipts for bank deposit planning.
  • Hourly Sales Heatmap: Identify peak store traffic hours to optimize cashier staffing schedules and counter openings.
  • Customer Loyalty Balance: Audit outstanding points liability, active members, and redemption rates.
