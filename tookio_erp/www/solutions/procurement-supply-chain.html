Complete Buying Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every procurement transaction type, governance control, and vendor report available in the ERPNext Buying module.

Procurement DocTypes & Transactions

  • Material Requests (Requisitions): Internal requests for Purchase, Transfer, Issue, or Manufacture with departmental approvals.
  • Request for Quotation (RFQ): Broadcast item requests to multiple vendors simultaneously with customizable delivery dates.
  • Supplier Quotation: Log vendor price bids, lead times, tax terms, and discounts with one-click conversion to Purchase Orders.
  • Purchase Orders & Blanket Orders: Create multi-currency POs with agreed delivery schedules, terms templates, and partial receipts.
  • Purchase Receipts (Goods Received Notes): Warehouse receiving against POs with accepted vs rejected quantities and quality sign-off.
  • Purchase Returns & Debit Notes: Return defective items back to vendors with direct debit note posting against payables.

Governance, Controls & Subcontracting

  • Automated 3-Way Matching: System matches PO items, quantities, and rates against physical GRN receipts and supplier invoices before payment release.
  • Multi-Level Approval Workflows: Route POs based on monetary thresholds (e.g. Dept Head < KES 50k, CFO < KES 500k, CEO > KES 500k).
  • Supplier Scorecard & Grading: Automatically score vendors on on-time delivery percentages, defect rates, and billing accuracy.
  • Subcontracting Workflow: Issue raw materials from inventory to third-party processors and receive finished goods with labor service costing.
  • Supplier Item Price Lists: Contracted vendor pricing rules that automatically lock unit costs on new Purchase Orders.
  • Purchase Budget Enforcement: Prevent departments from creating POs that exceed allocated monthly or quarterly project budgets.

Built-In Buying Reports

  • Purchase Analytics: Comprehensive spending breakdown by Supplier, Item Group, Brand, Cost Center, and Monthly trends.
  • Supplier Quotation Comparison: Side-by-side bid analysis matrix showing price variance, lead days, and terms.
  • Procurement Tracker: Real-time pipeline showing status from Material Request → RFQ → PO → Receipt → Invoice.
  • PO Items To Be Received: Open purchase order quantities still pending warehouse delivery by scheduled date.
  • Received Items To Be Billed: Physical GRNs accepted into inventory that have not yet had supplier bills posted.
  • Item-wise Purchase History: Historical price purchase trend per SKU across different vendors to optimize purchasing terms.
  • Subcontracted Items Status: Track raw materials held by external processors and pending finished deliveries.
