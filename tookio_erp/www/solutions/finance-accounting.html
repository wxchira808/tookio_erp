Complete Accounting Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every transaction type, automated control, and built-in financial statement available in the ERPNext Accounting suite.

Core DocTypes & Transactions

  • Chart of Accounts (Tree View): Hierarchical parent/child ledgers for Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income, and Expenses.
  • Journal Entries: General journals, opening balance entries, bank transfers, credit/debit adjustments, and auto-repeat recurring journals.
  • Sales Invoices: Automated double-entry debtor posting, tiered sales taxes, discounts, and one-click credit note refunds.
  • Purchase Invoices: Supplier bills linked to POs and GRNs, landed cost integration, and debit notes.
  • Payment Entries: Multi-currency receipts, supplier pay runs, bank-to-bank transfers, and unallocated advance matching.
  • Payment Requests & Dunning: Electronic payment link generation and automated multi-tier overdue dunning notices.

Controls, Banking & Governance

  • Bank Reconciliation Tool: Upload bank statements (CSV/OFX) to auto-match transactions against posted payment entries.
  • Accounting Dimensions & Cost Centers: Segment GL reports by Branch, Department, Project, or Sales Channel without bloating COA.
  • Budgeting & Variance Stops: Define monthly/annual cost center budgets with warn-or-block controls on Purchase Orders and expenses.
  • Deferred Accounting: Automatically amortize prepaid expenses (insurance/rent) and deferred subscription revenue across monthly periods.
  • Customer Credit Limit Controls: Hard credit limit rules that block quotations, sales orders, or shipments when customer limits are breached.
  • Tax Templates & Regional Compliance: Formula tax rules (VAT, Withholding Tax, Excise) and optional automated statutory e-invoicing.

Built-In Standard Reports

  • Balance Sheet: Real-time financial position with comparative date periods and multi-currency consolidation.
  • Profit and Loss Statement (P&L): Gross and net margins by month, quarter, cost center, or business unit.
  • Cash Flow Statement: Operating, investing, and financing cash flow analysis on direct and indirect methods.
  • General Ledger & Trial Balance: Complete audit trail with full voucher drill-down to underlying invoices and delivery notes.
  • Accounts Receivable & Payable Aging: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ day debtor and creditor aging summaries with contact details.
  • Gross Profit Analysis: Profit margins calculated per invoice, customer, item group, warehouse, or sales person.
  • Bank Reconciliation Statement: Cheques issued but not presented, deposits in transit, and cleared bank ledger balance.
