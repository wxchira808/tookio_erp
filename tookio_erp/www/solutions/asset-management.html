Complete Fixed Asset Architecture

Capital Work in Progress (CWIP)

Accumulate machine fabrication, building construction, and plant equipment installation costs in CWIP ledgers before capitalizing them into active depreciable assets.

  • One-click capitalization to fixed asset ledger

Complete Asset Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every asset DocType, depreciation rule, and maintenance report available in the ERPNext Asset suite.

Asset DocTypes & Movements

  • Asset Master: Detailed capital asset profile with purchase date, gross purchase amount, salvage value, and serial numbers.
  • Asset Categories: Group assets by class (Plant & Machinery, Vehicles, IT Hardware, Office Furniture) with pre-set GL accounts.
  • Asset Movement: Record physical custody transfers between staff, departments, or geographic branch offices.
  • Asset Value Adjustment: Revalue assets upwards or downwards based on formal valuation appraisals or impairment losses.
  • Asset Sale: Sell assets through a standard Sales Invoice with automated gain/loss on disposal calculations.
  • Asset Scrapping: Write off broken, obsolete, or damaged equipment with automated scrapping journal postings.

Depreciation & Maintenance Rules

  • Depreciation Methods: Support for Straight Line, Double Declining Balance, Written Down Value (WDV), and Manual entry.
  • Automated Monthly GL Posting: System scheduler automatically calculates and posts monthly depreciation entries without manual intervention.
  • Preventive Maintenance Tasks: Define maintenance checklists, service frequencies (Monthly, 500 operating hours), and maintenance teams.
  • Maintenance Logs & Repair Capitalization: Record technician inspection logs and capitalize major engine overhauls that extend useful asset life.
  • Asset Auto-Creation: Auto-create asset records directly upon submitting a Purchase Receipt for capital item groups.
  • Asset Insurance & Warranty Tracking: Log insurance policies, policy renewal dates, insurer contact, and warranty expiration.

Built-In Fixed Asset Reports

  • Fixed Asset Register: Complete statutory asset inventory showing item code, category, purchase date, cost, and net book value.
  • Asset Depreciation Ledger: Historical log of every depreciation journal entry posted per asset and accumulated depreciation balance.
  • Asset Depreciations and Balances: Summary of opening balance, additions, disposals, depreciation, and closing balance for financial audit.
  • Asset Maintenance Schedule: Overview of upcoming machine maintenance and calibration dates to avoid factory downtime.
  • Asset Maintenance Log Summary: Track maintenance expenditure, spare parts consumed, and technician labor per machine.
  • Asset Custodian Summary: Report of all company equipment assigned to each staff member for exit clearance.
