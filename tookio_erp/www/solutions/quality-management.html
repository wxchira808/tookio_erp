Complete Quality Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every inspection DocType, ISO compliance workflow, and defect analysis report available in the ERPNext Quality suite.

Quality DocTypes & Inspections

  • Quality Inspection Template: Standard parameter libraries specifying test criteria, numeric range limits, and measurement UOMs.
  • Quality Inspection Entry: Record sample inspection readings for Purchase Receipts, Job Cards, or Delivery Notes with pass/fail status.
  • Non-Conformance Report (NCR): Log defect occurrences, root-cause investigations, defect severities, and immediate containment measures.
  • Quality Action (CAPA): Assign Corrective and Preventive Actions with target resolution dates, responsible engineers, and audit reviews.
  • Quality Goals & Objectives: Document company-wide ISO quality objectives (e.g. Defect rate < 0.5%) and track progress against targets.
  • Quality Procedures & SOPs: Digital repository for standard operating procedures linked to relevant DocTypes and employee roles.

Governance & ISO Audit Tools

  • Mandatory Inspection Gates: Prevent warehouse receiving or customer dispatch if the required Quality Inspection has not passed.
  • Quality Review Cycles: Schedule recurring management reviews and internal quality audits with documented findings.
  • Customer Feedback & Complaints: Log customer defect claims and link them directly to the original production batch and work order.
  • Sampling Plans: Configure sample size algorithms (e.g. inspect 5% of incoming shipment or minimum 10 units per pallet).
  • Supplier Rejection Workflows: Automatically mark rejected lines on Purchase Receipts and generate debit memos against the vendor.
  • Audit Trail & Document Versioning: Complete revision tracking on SOPs and inspection templates to meet ISO 9001 requirements.

Built-In Quality Reports

  • Quality Inspection Summary: Review all completed inspections across items, warehouses, and inspectors with pass/fail statistics.
  • Supplier-Wise Rejection Rates: Audit percentage of rejected shipments per vendor to drive supplier contract reviews.
  • Non-Conformance Trends: Analyze defect causes by frequency and severity to eliminate recurring production bottlenecks.
  • Batch Quality Traceability: Complete backward and forward genealogy linking raw material inspection test results to finished goods batches.
  • CAPA Status Report: Monitor open corrective actions, overdue action items, and verification audit closures.
  • Customer Quality Claim Analysis: Track customer return defect trends by product line and manufacturing date.
