Complete Helpdesk Module Functionality & Standard Reports

Every support ticket DocType, SLA governance rule, and customer service report in the ERPNext Helpdesk suite.

Support DocTypes & Tickets

  • Issue / Ticket Master: Capture customer name, issue description, priority status (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), and assigned engineer.
  • Service Level Agreements (SLAs): Define response and resolution timelines based on working hours, holiday calendars, and client service tiers.
  • Warranty Claims: Look up original serial numbers and sales delivery notes to confirm active warranty coverage and authorize replacements.
  • Maintenance Schedules: Recurring service calendars for plant machinery, vehicles, and client equipment under annual maintenance contracts (AMC).
  • Maintenance Visit Records: Field technician report detailing work done, spare parts used, test runs, and customer sign-off signature.
  • Knowledge Base Articles: Create categorized self-service articles with rich media to help customers resolve issues independently.

Automation & Escalation Rules

  • Automated Email-to-Ticket Conversion: Configure support mailboxes to automatically generate tickets with attachments from incoming emails.
  • Rule-Based Ticket Routing: Assign incoming tickets to specific teams (Hardware, Software, Billing, Field Service) by keyword or customer account.
  • SLA Breach Escalations: Auto-escalate unresolved tickets to department managers before SLA breaches occur with email notifications.
  • Customer Satisfaction Surveys (CSAT): Send automatic rating requests upon ticket resolution to monitor agent performance.
  • Client Web Support Portal: Customers log in to view ticket status, reply to engineers, attach crash dumps, and close resolved tickets.
  • Spare Part Billing from Tickets: Withdraw replacement spare parts from warehouse stock and create a billing invoice in one click.

Built-In Helpdesk Reports

  • Issue Summary Report: Overview of all customer tickets by Status (Open, Replied, On Hold, Resolved, Closed) and Priority.
  • First Response Time for Issues: Audit average support response times against SLA targets by support engineer and customer tier.
  • SLA Fulfillment Rate: Percentage of support issues resolved within agreed contract windows across clients and teams.
  • Warranty Claim Summary: Track total warranty claims processed, replacement parts value, and defect causes.
  • Issue Analytics: Historical support volume trends by customer, product model, and root cause category.
  • Customer Feedback Analytics: Summary of CSAT satisfaction scores, net promoter ratings, and customer comments.
