Built for East African Enterprise Operations

Tookio Solutions is an enterprise business systems and ERP engineering partner based in Nairobi, Kenya. We help ambitious operating businesses transition from disconnected tools to centralized operational control.

We Understand Operations Before We Write Code

Too many software implementations fail because developers do not understand how a factory floor actually runs, how scrap is accounted for, or why accounts receivable reconciliation breaks down at month-end.

At Tookio, our engineers study your operational reality first. We walk your plant floor, map your supply chain dependencies, and configure ERPNext around your exact business model, regulatory environment, and team capabilities.

The Tookio Operating Mandate

  • Execution Over Buzzwords: We prioritize proven operational workflows, reliable double-entry bookkeeping, and audited stock controls.
  • Local Market Context: Native KRA eTIMS V2 compliance, local banking rails, and M-Pesa payment settlements built-in by design.
  • Transparent Ownership: Complete code, data, and architectural sovereignty with zero vendor lock-in.
